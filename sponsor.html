Support MoiCheck

MoiCheck is free, has no ads, and no part of it is behind a paywall. That is not going to change. If it helped you settle into Groningen and you feel like chipping in towards the running costs, this page is how.

Who's behind this

Hi — I'm Dor. I moved to Groningen as an expat myself, and MoiCheck started as the list I wished someone had handed me on day one: the 33 things that actually make you feel like a Stadjer, from a eierbal to a boat trip out of Lauwersoog.

It's a side project. There's no company behind it, no investor, no team — just me, evenings and weekends. The bucket-list items are picked because they're good, not because anyone paid for them to be there.

Where the money goes

MoiCheck runs cheaply on purpose, but it isn't free to keep online:

  • The moicheck.nl domain — roughly €10 a year.
  • Hosting (Vercel) — currently on a free plan.
  • Accounts & progress sync (Supabase) — currently on a free plan. If enough people sign in, this becomes about €25 a month.

So today the bill is small, and I cover it. Contributions go towards keeping it that way as more people use the app — and, if the free plans get outgrown, towards the monthly ones. If more comes in than the app costs, it goes towards making the app better: more items, better maps, more of the province beyond the city.

What it does and doesn't get you

Nothing, on purpose — and that's the point. A contribution is a thank-you, not a purchase:

  • Every feature stays free for everyone. Nothing is unlocked by paying, and nothing ever will be.
  • You can't buy a spot on the list. Items are chosen on merit. If a business ever pays for placement, it will be labelled as such — but the plan is that it never happens.
  • No perks, badges, or supporter tiers. That would make this a purchase rather than a gift, which changes what it is (and drags in VAT and admin I'd rather not spend the evenings on).
  • Suggestions are always welcome and always free. Use the contact form — a contribution doesn't move your suggestion up the queue.

And to be very clear: please don't feel any obligation. Using the app, telling a friend who just landed here, or sending me a good item for the list is worth just as much.

Payment & privacy

Payments are handled entirely by Stripe on Stripe's own checkout page. Your card or bank details never touch MoiCheck — I never see them and never store them. What I do see, in Stripe's dashboard, is the amount, the date, and the name and email you give at checkout, which I keep for my own bookkeeping.

Contributing doesn't create a MoiCheck account and isn't linked to your bucket-list progress. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
